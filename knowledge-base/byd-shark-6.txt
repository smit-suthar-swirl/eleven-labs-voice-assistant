BYD SHARK 6 - COMPLETE SPECIFICATIONS AND FEATURES

OVERVIEW:
The BYD Shark 6 is a revolutionary plug-in hybrid electric pickup truck (PHEV) that combines exceptional power, efficiency, and advanced technology. It represents BYD's entry into the competitive pickup truck market with a focus on sustainability and performance.

ENGINE & PERFORMANCE:
- Powertrain: Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicle (PHEV)
- System Power: 430 HP combined (321 kW)
- Torque: 650 Nm
- Acceleration: 0-100 km/h in 5.7 seconds
- Top Speed: 160 km/h (electronically limited)
- Electric Range: 100 km in pure EV mode
- Total Range: 800+ km with full battery and fuel tank
- Fuel Consumption: 7.5L/100km (combined cycle)

DRIVETRAIN & SUSPENSION:
- Drive Type: Intelligent 4WD (Four-Wheel Drive)
- Electronic Differential Lock
- Driving Modes: EV Only, Hybrid, Sport, Snow, Mud, Sand, Rock
- Front Suspension: Double wishbone independent
- Rear Suspension: Multi-link independent
- Ground Clearance: 230 mm

BATTERY & CHARGING:
- Battery Type: Blade Battery (LFP - Lithium Iron Phosphate)
- Battery Capacity: 29.6 kWh
- AC Charging: 3-4 hours (7kW charger)
- DC Fast Charging: 30 minutes to 80% (60kW)
- Vehicle-to-Load (V2L): 3.3 kW output for external devices

TOWING & PAYLOAD CAPACITY:
- Maximum Towing Capacity: 2,500 kg (braked)
- Payload Capacity: 835 kg
- Bed Length: 1,500 mm

- Tailgate: Electric release with dampened opening
